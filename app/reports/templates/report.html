How to use this report
1. Open the official tender.
Confirm conditions of participation, mandatory experience, insurance, security, location and response requirements.
2. Make a bid/no-bid decision.
Use the NixSec relevance evidence as a prioritisation aid, not as a substitute for the tender documentation.
3. Track what changes.
Monitor addenda, closing-date changes, newly published opportunities and relevant contract expiry signals.
4. Refine the profile.
Confirmed capabilities, excluded work, preferred agencies and contract-value limits make future matching materially more precise.
Evidence standard. Live opportunities in this report must be recently observed in the official current ATM feed and pass NixSec's deterministic relevance gate. Company identity fields are source-record fields unless explicitly marked as independently verified. AI may explain and prioritise supplied facts, but it is not permitted to invent tender facts, supplier history, eligibility, scores or dates.
